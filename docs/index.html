Quick guide

Welcome to the table

  • The host shares the six-character table code or invite link.
  • Players join through the relay, with no connection-code exchange or network setup.
  • Import a Tabletop Simulator save or saved-object JSON file.
  • Click the draw pile to add a private card to your hand.
  • Hover over a card and hold Z to zoom it. Press F to flip it.
  • Drag a card or stack directly onto another card to combine them.
  • Hover over a stack and press S to shuffle it or D to deal it.
  • Drag table cards to arrange them or drop the top card on your hand to take it back.
  • Clicking a card marks it with your player color; click empty table space to clear it.
  • Use the chat button in the header; chat can be minimized at any time.
