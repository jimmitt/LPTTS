Quick guide

Welcome to the table

  • The current player uses End turn → to pass the turn to the next player.
  • Import a TTS deck, create an image deck, or add standard playing cards. Each appears as a stack on the table.
  • Hover over a card and hold Z to zoom it. Press F to flip only the top card.
  • Press Shift+F over a stack to flip its top card and match every card’s orientation.
  • Press H over a table card to take it, or over a hand card to play it in front of you.
  • Left-drag a stack to move only its top card; right-drag to move the whole stack. Cards stack only with more than 85% overlap.
  • Drag a selected group of cards onto a stack to add the whole group.
  • Press O repeatedly over a stack or fan to cycle down, left, up, and right layouts.
  • Hover over a stack and press S to shuffle it or D to deal it.
  • Control-drag a rectangle over the table to select multiple cards and dice.
  • Left-drag dice to move them; right-drag to move and roll. Press S to roll selected dice.
  • Use Delete or the trash icon to delete every selected item. Open the trash with nothing selected to choose items to restore.
  • Use the mouse wheel to zoom; hold the middle mouse button and drag to pan.
