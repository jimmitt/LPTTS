Quick guide

Welcome to the table

  • The host shares the six-character table code or invite link.
  • Import a Tabletop Simulator save or saved-object JSON file.
  • Hover over a card and hold Z to zoom it. Press F to flip only the top card.
  • Press Shift+F over a stack to flip its top card and match every card’s orientation.
  • Press H over a table card to take it, or over a hand card to play it in front of you.
  • Left-drag a stack to move only its top card; right-drag to move the whole stack.
  • Drop a card or stack directly onto another card to combine them.
  • Left-click a stack to select its top card; right-click to select the whole stack.
  • Hover over a stack and press S to shuffle it or D to deal it.
  • Add dice beside the draw pile; hover over a die and press S to roll it.
  • Select a card, stack, or die and use the trash icon to destroy it.
  • Use the mouse wheel to zoom; middle-button drag the tabletop to pan.
